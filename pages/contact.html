FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find the answer you're looking for? Our team is happy to help — reach out directly and we'll respond within one business day.

How long does a typical architectural project take?

Project timelines vary greatly depending on scale and complexity. A high-end residential home may take 12–18 months from concept to handover, while a large commercial tower can take 3–5 years. We always provide a detailed programme at the start of each engagement.

What is included in the Client Portal?

The client portal gives you access to all project documents and blueprints, a 3D model viewer, the live milestone schedule, an approval workflow engine, and a direct messaging channel to your project team — all in one secure, cloud-based dashboard.

Do you handle international projects?

Yes. We have active studios in New York, London, and Dubai, and we have delivered projects across 18 countries. Our team is experienced in navigating international building codes, planning systems, and contractor markets.

Can I commission 3D renderings without full architectural services?

Absolutely. Our visualisation studio accepts standalone commissions for CGI, virtual tours, and animation films. We can work from your existing drawings, BIM models, or concept sketches to produce photorealistic imagery to your brief.

What is your approach to sustainable design?

Sustainability is integrated into every project from day one — not added as an afterthought. We target LEED, BREEAM, and WELL certifications, and our team includes dedicated green design specialists who advise on passive energy systems, biophilic design, and low-carbon materials.
