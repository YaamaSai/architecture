Our Story

Forging the Future
of Architecture

Founded in 2010 by a collective of award-winning architects and structural engineers, ArchStruct has grown into one of the most trusted design firms in the world — delivering landmark projects across 18 countries.

Our Mission

Architecture That Stands the Test of Time.

At ArchStruct, we believe every structure tells a story. Our mission is to write those stories with a combination of artistic vision, rigorous engineering, and a deep respect for the environment and communities we build within.

We don't just design buildings — we create environments that elevate the human experience. From the first concept sketch to the final handover key, our interdisciplinary team ensures every detail is considered, every system is optimised, and every client is heard.

Award-Winning Design Methodology

Our design process has been recognized by the AIA, RIBA, and the Pritzker Architecture Prize committee.

Sustainability at the Core

We integrate passive energy systems, biophilic design, and low-carbon materials into every project from day one.

Technology-Driven Collaboration

Our proprietary client portal ensures full transparency — every blueprint, 3D model, and milestone is accessible in real time.

Our Journey

A Decade and a Half of Design Excellence.

From a two-architect studio in Brooklyn to a globally recognised firm, every milestone has been built on the principles of innovation, integrity, and impact.

2010 — Founded
ArchStruct Opens Its Doors

Robert Hughes and Elena Rostova establish a boutique architecture studio in Brooklyn, New York, with a focus on residential design and structural innovation.

2013 — First Commercial Win
The Vertex Office Complex

Our first major commercial commission — a 12-floor office complex in Midtown Manhattan — earns our first AIA New York Citation of Merit.

2016 — International Expansion
London & Dubai Offices Open

Growing demand from European and Middle Eastern developers leads to the opening of our international studios, expanding our project portfolio across 8 new countries.

2020 — Tech Transformation
Client Portal Launches

We launch our proprietary client collaboration portal, enabling real-time document sharing, 3D model review, and milestone tracking — redefining client engagement in the industry.

2026 — Present
250+ Projects, 18 Countries

Today, ArchStruct is a team of 85+ architects, engineers, and visualisation specialists delivering landmark architecture on six continents.
