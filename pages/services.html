What We Do

Full-Spectrum
Design & Engineering

From the first concept to the final inspection certificate, our integrated team of architects, engineers, and visualisation experts deliver with absolute precision across every discipline.

01
Discipline One

Architectural Design

Our architectural design practice is built on a foundation of rigorous research, creative exploration, and deep technical expertise. We believe that the best buildings are those that seamlessly integrate with their context while pushing the boundaries of what is possible.

From intimate residential commissions to large-scale urban mixed-use developments, our design team crafts spaces that are functional, inspiring, and enduring. Every project begins with a thorough understanding of your objectives, your users, and the site — and ends with a building that exceeds your expectations.

Conceptual Design Schematic Design Design Development Construction Docs Master Planning Interior Architecture
02
Discipline Two

Structural Engineering

Beneath every great building lies a great structure. Our structural engineering team uses the most advanced computational analysis tools available — including finite element modelling, parametric design, and BIM integration — to engineer frameworks that are simultaneously robust, efficient, and elegant.

We have extensive experience in complex structural challenges including high-rise construction, long-span structures, seismic design, foundation engineering in difficult ground conditions, and structural retrofitting of heritage buildings.

Seismic Design High-Rise Systems FEA Analysis Foundation Engineering Wind Load Studies Structural Retrofitting
03
Discipline Three

3D Visualization & Rendering

Our visualisation studio produces cinema-quality 3D imagery, immersive virtual reality experiences, and animated walkthroughs that bring your project to life long before the first shovel hits the ground. We use photorealistic rendering engines, custom material libraries, and drone simulation to create images that are indistinguishable from photography.

Whether you need investor-grade presentation materials, planning application imagery, or a fully interactive VR experience for a sales centre, our visualisation team delivers with speed and absolute quality.

Exterior CGI Interior CGI 360° Walkthroughs VR Experiences Drone Simulation Animation Films
04
Discipline Four

Project Management & Secure Portal

Managing a complex construction project requires exceptional coordination. Our dedicated project management team acts as your single point of contact — coordinating architects, engineers, contractors, and consultants to keep your project on time, on budget, and on brief.

Every client is onboarded onto our proprietary collaboration portal, giving you 24/7 access to your project's blueprints, 3D models, approval workflows, milestone schedule, and a direct communication channel to your project team.

Programme Management Cost Control Contractor Coordination Blueprint Management 3D Model Reviews Real-Time Reporting
Access Client Portal
05
Discipline Five

Interior Design

Our interior design team believes that the spaces we inhabit have a profound impact on our well-being and productivity. We design bespoke interiors that are visually stunning, deeply functional, and perfectly aligned with the architectural shell.

From material selection to custom furniture design and spatial planning, we ensure every detail contributes to a cohesive narrative.

Space Planning Material Selection Custom Furniture Lighting Design Acoustic Strategy FF&E Procurement
06
Discipline Six

Landscape Architecture

We view landscape architecture as an essential extension of the building itself. Our landscape designs blur the boundaries between indoors and outdoors, creating ecological, social, and aesthetic value.

Whether designing a corporate courtyard or a large public park, our landscapes are highly resilient and beautifully crafted.

Site Planning Hardscape Design Planting Strategy Water Management Public Plazas Rooftop Gardens
07
Discipline Seven

Sustainable & Green Design

Sustainability is not an add-on; it is embedded in everything we do. We utilize passive design strategies, advanced energy modeling, and low-impact materials to minimize environmental footprints.

We actively guide our clients through global certification frameworks (LEED, BREEAM, WELL) to deliver buildings that perform exceptionally well and benefit the planet.

LEED Consulting Energy Modeling Passive Design Life Cycle Analysis Carbon Reduction WELL Standards
08
Discipline Eight

Urban Planning & Masterplanning

Our urban planning team tackles projects at the neighborhood and city scales. We focus on mobility, density, zoning, and public realm quality to create masterplans that serve generations.

We work closely with municipalities and developers to chart the future growth of complex urban environments.

Masterplanning Urban Regeneration Zoning Studies Mobility Planning Public Realm Feasibility Analysis
How We Work

Our Proven 4-Step
Design Process

Every ArchStruct project follows a carefully structured process designed to ensure clarity, efficiency, and excellence from the very first meeting.

01

Discovery & Brief

Deep-dive consultation to fully understand your vision, budget, timeline, and the technical constraints of the site and programme.

02

Concept & Design

Our architects develop multiple concept directions, refined through design reviews and supported by mood boards, sketches, and 3D visualisations.

03

Engineering & Documentation

Full structural analysis, MEP coordination, and the production of complete construction documentation packages for planning and contractor tender.

04

Construction & Handover

On-site construction administration, contractor oversight, quality inspection, and a complete project handover with as-built documentation.
