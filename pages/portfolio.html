Featured Case Study

Nexus Corporate Tower — A New York Landmark

The 62-floor Nexus Corporate Tower on Manhattan's west side is one of ArchStruct's most technically demanding and visually iconic projects. The programme includes 85,000 sq ft of prime office space, a public sky garden on the 32nd floor, ground-floor retail, and an underground transit connection.

The tower's distinctive diagrid structural system — engineered in collaboration with our structural team — allowed the elimination of internal columns across entire floors, creating uninterrupted open-plan spaces with 360° skyline views.
